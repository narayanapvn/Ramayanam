# Content replacement
Add episodes to the `episodes` list in `MainActivity.kt`. Each item must include a stable ID, Kanda, title, exact Sarga reference, approximately 20-minute script in Telugu/Hindi/English, and an editorial status. Do not insert copied modern translations. Keep evidence of source rights and reviewer approval outside the public repository.

A natural female voice can be selected by the user in Android Settings > Text-to-speech output if that language's voice pack is installed. The app deliberately does not bundle or clone a person's voice.
